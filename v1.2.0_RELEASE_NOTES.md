# v1.2.0 — corrected mitochondrial calls and DeltaALT uncertainty release

This release supersedes v1.1.1 for manuscript citation. It corrects a
mitochondrial-only workflow defect: three overlapping read-set states had been
merged as if independent. The corrected workflow uses one combined a--c,
collapsed-only, deduplicated full-UDG BAM per individual and explicitly rejects
the nested `.2` and `.3` supersets.

It also aligns the public package with Reduce18F by adding the Round45 DeltaALT
uncertainty analysis and the LOCO 95% intervals plotted in Figure 2.

## Corrected results

- ORT15 and ORT16 remain assigned to D4o1.
- HaploGrep 3 scores remain 0.8911 and 0.9107.
- Corrected caller-emitted SNP counts are 36 for ORT15 and 37 for ORT16.
- All 36 ORT15 calls match ORT16 exactly by chromosome, position, REF and ALT.
- Historical ORT15 A4215G does not survive; neither corrected input has
  qualifying coverage at position 4215.
- ORT16 C7028T remains a caller-emitted call supported by one forward read
  (MAPQ 37, base quality 40) and is not a confirmed pairwise difference.
- Both VCFs contain `MT:3106 CN>C`, deletion of the artificial rCRS N spacer;
  it is excluded from biological SNP and discordance counts.

The mitochondrial defect does not affect autosomal PMD correction, imputation,
ancIBD, kinship, PCA, manuscript figures, numbered manuscript tables, or the
separate non-UDG mtCont contamination analysis. Figure 2 is updated separately
to display the author-approved DeltaALT uncertainty intervals.

## DeltaALT uncertainty addition

- 26 correction-state LOCO intervals support Figure 2.
- 90 primary paired contrasts cover LOCO, 5-Mb and 10-Mb block schemes.
- 24 common-callable sensitivity contrasts document site-set dependence.
- The validator confirms the two raw library-type LOCO effects, every
  correction-versus-uncorrected interval direction, the three state intervals
  crossing zero, and the common-callable sensitivity conclusions.
- Full per-site allele-count TSVs remain external; compact summaries and
  sanitized source checksums are included without server paths.

## Reproducibility changes

- `workflows/09_mtdna/run_haplogroup.sh` now accepts one canonical BAM per sample.
- `validate_manifest.py` rejects nested read-set states and duplicate sample/BAM
  rows and verifies the known per-sample canonical input SHA-256.
- `summarize_target_sites.py` emits aggregate evidence without read names.
- `summarize_calls.py` compares exact SNP alleles and keeps no coverage distinct
  from a reference genotype.
- Compact corrected classifications, concordance, target-site and rCRS-spacer
  summaries replace the stale v1.1.1 tables.
- Public unit tests reconstruct the corrected summary tables from synthetic VCFs
  and reject invalid nested-state manifests.
- The obsolete Figure 1 SVG/Matplotlib generator has been retired. Figure 1 is
  an author-prepared PowerPoint schematic maintained outside this repository;
  automated figure reproduction now covers data-derived main Figures 2–7.

The 36/37 values are linear-rCRS caller-emitted counts, not claims of complete
breakpoint-independent mitogenomes. BAMs, VCFs and reference/tool resources remain
outside the public repository; path-neutral checksums identify the validated
inputs and outputs.

Historical v1.1.0 and v1.1.1 records are retained unchanged.
